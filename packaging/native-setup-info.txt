MY CAREER TRAINING — WINDOWS TEST

This installs one app and its support files. There is no ZIP to extract.
After setup, open the My Career Training shortcut. Its helpers start automatically.

You need your installed Steam copy of Rocket League. Close the game before opening
My Career Training. This test supports only the game build listed in its release notes.

BakkesMod connects My Career to Rocket League. If it is missing, the app offers
guided setup using the official BakkesMod installer. It is more than a skins tool.

My Career uses Rocket League's supported offline mode. Online matchmaking is not
part of the career. Close My Career before starting the normal game from Steam.
If Rocket League asks whether to view instructions for enabling Easy Anti-Cheat,
choose No to dismiss those instructions and continue this offline session.

This is a test release. See its release notes for what was actually tested.
Setup and uninstall keep your separate My Career saves.
