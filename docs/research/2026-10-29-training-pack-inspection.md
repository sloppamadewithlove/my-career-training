# Actual training-pack inspection — 29 October 2026

Question: can the three existing downloaded training files supply the owner-selected combined Poquito Ground Shots / Skogur Powershot Practice Gold tier?

Result: **no**. A bounded in-memory metadata reader identified three other share codes with 13, 10 and 9 rounds. None matches Poquito `6EB1-79B2-33B8-681C` or Skogur `C9E4-0F05-B71A-C322`. All three encrypted-envelope CRC checks passed, format magic/object-table bounds were validated, and each original file's SHA256 was identical before and after inspection. No original file was modified and no decrypted payload, account information or game asset was published.

This was metadata-only inspection, not validated shot-geometry extraction. The implementation read the training object's Code, TM_Name and Rounds array count; it did not treat arbitrary strings as verified pack identity. Unknown cached packs are not replacements for the owner's selected references.
