| Rebound | Freeplay only. | Dribble | Freeplay only. Priority adapter candidates

1. BallPrediction: strongest read-only overlay candidate. First establish the actual lesson context, plugin identity, toggle readback, event support, drawable cleanup, and whether global drawable unregistration interferes with other overlays.
2. Mechanical: optional Freeplay-only input modifier for lessons that explicitly want input clamping/holding. Snapshot and restore the exact prior `mech_*` values; verify that user control and lesson scoring still behave as intended.
3. Redirect, Rebound, Dribble: consider only as deliberate one-shot lesson actions whose scenario controller remains the sole authority for timing and calls the ball seed at a defined transition.
4. Defer Defender, Training, Workshop, and Recovery to separate practice modes unless the lesson is deliberately designed around their ball/car ownership and timing behavior.
