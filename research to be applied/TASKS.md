## Hourly checkpoint contract

The local monitor hashes sanitized research documents and records actual UTC time,
changed paths, missed checks and the final window end. Main checks worker progress,
new evidence, source freshness, privacy and publication. Document changes alone do
not prove that a worker ran. No inactive interval is relabeled as completed research.
Codex scheduling tools were unavailable; the local monitor cannot wake a closed app.

## Working boundaries

Keep the existing game-input owner and loaded components undisturbed. Use isolated
code experiments only when they answer a concrete research question, with separate
DeepSeek build/review and appropriate lightweight checks. Heavy native builds stay
deferred while parallel timing/physics activity is unknown. Do not claim account
item ownership, online mod support, successful map arrival or verified instruction
traces from static declarations or UI controls alone.
